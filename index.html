About Harriet

Forty years at the dining room door

Harriet is eighty-four. For forty years she stood at the door of a small country hotel dining room and watched thousands of men walk in. She noticed their shoes before their faces, and how they spoke to the waiters before how they spoke to their guests.

Now she writes to her grandsons — and to you — one rule at a time.

Please note: Harriet Pembridge is an AI-generated character created by Pembridge Press. Her story is fiction. The guidance in our workbooks is general advice on manners, grooming and dress, and is not professional, medical, legal or financial advice.
